Plinko is easy to recognise: a ball falls through a board of pins and settles in a multiplier slot. What deserves more attention is the amount attached to each drop. A R10 stake can return more or less than R10, depending on the multiplier reached.

The ball starts at the top, bounces left and right through the pins and eventually settles into one of the multiplier slots below. If it lands on 2x, the round returns R20. If it finds 0.5x, only R5 comes back.

That's almost everything you need to know to understand your first round.

But it's not everything you need to know before betting real rands.

Depending on the version of Plinko you're playing, you may also be choosing the number of rows, a Low, Medium or High risk setting and the size of every drop. Those controls can change the board considerably, while the stake determines how quickly those few-second rounds affect your balance.

So before we start chasing the big multipliers sitting at the edges, let's actually play with the board.

Plinko explained: the board, pins and multiplier slots

Instead of reels and paylines, Plinko uses a vertical board filled with pins.

The round moves in one direction:

top → pins → bottom

You place a bet before the ball is released. It then moves through the pegboard until it reaches one of the multiplier positions at the bottom, and the result is calculated according to the rules of that game.

In its simplest form:

Choose settings → Bet → Drop → Multiplier → Result

There's no need to learn dozens of symbols before your first round. You can watch one ball and immediately understand the basic idea.

The settings around the board are where things get more interesting.

If you've opened the game and wondered where the reels are, nothing is missing. The board has replaced them.

What is Plinko?

Plinko is a drop-style game built around a pegboard and multiplier slots. A ball travels through rows of pins and finishes in one of the multiplier positions at the bottom, which determines the round's return according to the rules of that version.

Can I play Plinko online in South Africa?

Is Plinko a slot game?

It's frequently grouped with slots and instant casino games, although it doesn't use conventional spinning reels. Plinko presents the result through a ball moving across a pegboard towards multiplier slots.

How does Plinko work?

You select the available settings, choose a stake and start the round. The ball travels down the board and reaches a multiplier that determines the return under that game's rules.

What does Low, Medium or High risk change?

Risk settings can alter the distribution and range of multipliers in versions that support them. Higher-risk configurations may offer more extreme potential outcomes, but that doesn't mean they're automatically better.

What do the rows do in Plinko?

Changing the number of rows can alter the board and multiplier distribution. The exact effect depends on the particular Plinko implementation you're using.

Is there a Plinko app for South Africa?

Is Plinko legit in South Africa?

Is High risk the best setting?
